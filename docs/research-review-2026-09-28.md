# External Resource Review — 2026-09-28

This review records bounded protocol clarifications, source decisions, and dated upstream observations from fourteen resources.

The additions to Skill Lifecycle §§12–13, Active Workspace §15.1, and Memory §23.3 are design-only explanations of existing evidence rules. They add no admission gate, schema, level, dependency, or required evaluator. The protocols retain `practiced` maturity on their existing evidence; this review supplies no local benefit or conformance validation.

The reading scope below covers the named documents, including every listed appendix. Source inspection covers the relevant paths, not entire repositories. Downloading, reading, inspecting code, and running it are distinct: no upstream benchmark, device behavior, vulnerability, or logged-in service was reproduced. Paper figures were read through captions and tables, with image inspection where stated; this is not a claim to have inspected every experimental screenshot pixel by pixel.
